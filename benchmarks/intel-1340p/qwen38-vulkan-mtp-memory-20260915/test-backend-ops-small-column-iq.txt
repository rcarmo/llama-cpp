Measurement-only patch used during the 14-row small-column IQ performance diagnosis. It adds N=1 and N=7 projection shapes for IQ1_S, IQ1_M, IQ2_XXS, IQ2_XS, IQ2_S, IQ3_XXS and IQ3_S at m=17408, k=5120. The matrix identified IQ2_S as the main Intel Vulkan gap; all tested broad tiled substitutions were slower. This patch is not part of the production runtime commit.
